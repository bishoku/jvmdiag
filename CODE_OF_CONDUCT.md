# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive and patient. Discuss technical ideas and evidence without personal attacks. Welcome contributors regardless of background, identity, experience or employer. Respect privacy and different communication styles.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, spam and deliberate publication of another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not use diagnostic artifacts to expose customer or contributor data.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions and other project-managed spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, pause discussions, warn participants or restrict participation. They should explain enforcement decisions privately when practical and apply the policy consistently.

Contact a maintainer privately through the contact options on their GitHub profile to report an incident. If no private channel is listed, request one without describing the incident publicly. Do not post private reports or identifying details in an issue. Maintainers should disclose conflicts of interest and involve another maintainer when available.
